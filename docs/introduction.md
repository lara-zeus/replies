## Introduction
@zeus Replies Add the ability to associate comments to your FilamentPHP app

**[Demo](https://demo.larazeus.com/admin/tickets/1) · [Github](https://github.com/lara-zeus/replies) · [Discord](https://discord.com/channels/883083792112300104/1345513165122834472)**

## Features

- 🔥 Associate comments to any model.
- 🔥 The comments can be approved and nested.
- 🔥 Included Livewire component
- 🔥 More Features are coming
